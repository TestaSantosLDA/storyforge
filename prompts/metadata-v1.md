## What to produce
- title: curiosity-driven and searchable, 50–60 characters, includes the main character's name or the series name. No studio, franchise or existing character names.
- hook: the first two lines of the description, which show in search. Make someone want to watch.
- summary: two or three sentences about the story, without spoiling the ending.
- moral_line: one short sentence naming the lesson.
- chapter_titles: one short title per scene above, in order (2–5 words each).
- tags: 8–15 search tags, most specific first.
- hashtags: up to 3, each starting with #.
- thumbnails: exactly 3, each a shot id from the list and 2–4 words of overlay text that make someone curious. Pick three different shots with a clear main character.
- The audience is families watching together; the channel is "not made for kids". Present it as family entertainment.
