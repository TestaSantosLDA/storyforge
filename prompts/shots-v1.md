## Rules
- Split each scene into shots at line boundaries. Every line belongs to exactly one shot, in script order; a shot never spans two scenes.
- Aim for {{min_seconds}} to {{max_seconds}} seconds per shot: add up the seconds of its lines. A single line longer than {{max_seconds}} seconds may be a shot on its own.
- At most {{max_characters}} characters per shot, chosen only from the cast above (narrator is not a character). A shot may show no characters, e.g. a landscape.
- For each character in a shot, give one short sentence saying what that character is doing, naming them ("Tamsin holds up the jam-jar lantern."). Keep it to what is visible in a single still picture.
- Mention each prop in only one sentence of the shot (e.g. the lantern only in the action of whoever holds it): the image model draws a separate copy for every mention.
- setting: the place and light for this shot in one sentence, consistent with the scene's visual description.
- framing: wide, medium or close_up. Vary it across the episode; use close_up for emotional moments.
- camera: zoom_in, zoom_out, pan_left, pan_right or hold. Slow and gentle; vary it.
- Shot ids are "SH01", "SH02", ... in order.
