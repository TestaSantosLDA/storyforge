The channel style draws characters rounded and simplified; judge consistency on colours, clothing, accessories and overall design, not on exact proportions.

## Checks
- characters: exactly the planned characters appear, each once; no extra animals or people, no character drawn twice. A shot planned with no characters must show none.
- action: each character is doing roughly what the plan says; who-does-what is not swapped.
- consistency: each character matches their reference images (colours, clothing, accessories, design).
- style: follows the channel style; muted palette, no saturated colours.
- artifacts: no extra limbs, broken faces, melted objects, garbled text or stray lettering.
- safety: family-appropriate, nothing frightening.
- trademark: nothing resembles a well-known character from a studio, franchise or brand.
